2026-08-30 — My Restaurant screen (2nd change of the day)

A "My Restaurant" screen for the owner that shows every detail of their
restaurant, or an empty state when nothing's been added yet. It's reached
from the Menu tab of the bottom navbar, which used to open the unfinished
Add Dish screen.

The screen

Data comes from getRestaurantInfo() (POST /api/get-restaurant-info/),
reloaded on every focus with useFocusEffect so edits made on
RestaurantInformationScreen show immediately.

Three states:
- loading            -> spinner
- no restaurant yet  -> store icon, "No information yet", and an
                        "Add Restaurant Information" button
- restaurant present -> cover photo (or a placeholder), the logo
                        overlapping it, name + description, a Contact card
                        (address, contact number, email), a Location card
                        (city, province, region, country, lat, long), an
                        "Edit Information" button, and an "Added on <date>"
                        footnote from created_at

Blank fields render a muted "Not provided" so nothing is silently hidden.
A small DetailRow component renders each label/value pair; formatDate()
turns created_at into a readable date. The stylesheet reuses the existing
colour tokens and the Ezra font.
