add reviews, ratings and notifications; rebuild both home screens

Fills in three apps that existed as models only (review, ranking,
notification) and gives the app enough sample data to see them working.
Both home screens are rebuilt around what comes out.

Sample data
- new seed_sample_data command: 15 restaurants around Northern Mindanao
  with real coordinates, each with an owner account, location, 7-day
  opening hours, cuisine categories, sometimes a branch, and 6 dishes,
  plus 8 diner accounts. Idempotent on natural keys; --fresh wipes only
  the sample rows. 90 dishes, every restaurant carrying at least 5.
- the dataset lives in _sample_data.py, shared with seed_reviews; the
  leading underscore keeps it out of Django's command discovery.
- restaurant migration 0002 drops NOT NULL from operating_hours
  opening_time/closing_time. The model has said null=True since
  0001_initial but the live database predated it, so a restaurant closed
  on a Sunday could not be saved. Has to be RunSQL - an AlterField emits
  nothing when the model state already matches.

Reviews and ratings
- review/queries.py: get/add/delete for restaurant and dish reviews, plus
  getMyReviews for a diner and getOwnerReviews for an owner. A restaurant
  is scored on food, service and ambiance with overall as their mean; a
  dish gets one rating.
- reviews upsert, one per account per subject, so a second submission
  edits rather than stacks. Deletes are scoped to the author.
- summaries carry a stars_5..stars_1 histogram via COUNT(*) FILTER, so a
  screen draws its bars without a second request.
- posting a review inserts the owner's notification on the same cursor and
  transaction, skipped when an owner reviews their own restaurant.
- WriteReviewScreen is one page for both kinds, switched on route params,
  and pre-fills from the diner's existing review. New DinerRestaurant,
  DinerDish and RestaurantReviews screens for diners, OwnerReviews for
  owners.

Notifications
- notification/queries.py, all scoped by account_id, so the same endpoints
  serve owners and diners and who you are decides what comes back.
- NotificationList holds the whole feed - load, pull-to-refresh, optimistic
  mark-read, mark-all - leaving each role's screen a thin wrapper.

Home screens
- ranking/queries.py: top restaurants, top dishes, nearby restaurants.
  Bayesian score with a 3-review prior, so two 5-star reviews don't
  outrank forty 4.8s.
- the top lists rank across all of goodFood rather than the user's
  surroundings. Clipping to a radius meant a device outside Northern
  Mindanao saw three empty sections. Distance is still computed and shown
  on each card, but only narrows the list when a radius is asked for;
  getNearbyRestaurants keeps its 40km default since that is its purpose.
- distance is haversine in SQL. Coordinates are free text and inconsistent
  ("~8.229° N" alongside "8.2280"), so every read strips non-numerics
  before casting and unparseable rows drop out rather than failing.
- both homes render the shared TopPicks: Top Dishes, Top Restaurants and
  Latest Reviews. Headings render while loading - the first version showed
  only a spinner, which read as the sections being missing.
- dropped the "Near {city}" header label; it came from the top-ranked
  result's city, which means nothing once ranking is app-wide. That slot
  now prompts for location only when it is off.

Sample reviews
- new seed_reviews command: 94 restaurant reviews, 341 dish reviews and
  131 notifications across 15 owners and 8 diners. Each restaurant has a
  house quality and ratings are drawn around it, so the top lists have a
  real spread.
- built in memory and written with bulk_create + bulk_update; a row-at-a-
  time version timed out past two minutes against the remote database.
  bulk_update is also what lets a backdated created_at survive
  auto_now_add, since it skips pre_save.

Checked: Django check clean, no migration drift, every endpoint exercised
through the test client against the live database with no regressions.
Ranking verified from Iligan, Cagayan de Oro, Manila and with no location.
ESLint clean.

NOT verified: none of this has been run in the app. No simulator or device
build this session, so the screens and navigation need a real run before
they can be called done.
